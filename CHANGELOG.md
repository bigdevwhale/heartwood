# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-22

The first release.

- Befriend real trees: a photo turned into an ink engraving, the species from an illustrated key (twelve species), the girth from a hug and the age by the forester's rule, a name, a line and a point on an OpenStreetMap map.
- The friendship as a sawn cross-section: a ring for every day you meet, in the season's colour, warm for a hug, a dark line after a long absence; tap a ring and the tree remembers the day and its weather.
- Only at the tree (within 50 m): a hug with a slowing heartbeat (proximity sensor and stillness), ten seconds of the bark's voice, a bark rubbing lifted from a photo of the real bark, a pressed leaf a season, a photo fitted over the last for a timelapse chronicle, sitting beside it face down, and a note in the hollow that opens a year later.
- Friendship levels from stranger to kindred, by meetings, seasons and a full year.
- A grove of up to seven trees on a sepia map, with the mycelium underground carrying news from the friend met last.
- Rare calls from the trees (blossom, red leaves, first snow, a year since you met, a ripe note), twice a month at most.
- Moss instead of guilt; pass a tree on to a friend with a QR code.
- The forester's notebook: herbarium, rubbings, voices and hollows. English and Russian.
